## Catalog behavior
- Course cards are generated from the LocalStorage course records.
- Search matches course title, description, instructor, category, module titles, and lesson/topic titles.
- Category and difficulty filters operate on course records.
- Popular sorting uses actual enrollment records, not preset popularity numbers.
- Ratings are shown as unavailable until reviews exist; no fabricated review count is displayed.
- Course detail pages show actual enrollment counts and curriculum counts.
- Course enrollments and lesson completion continue to use the existing LearnX services.
